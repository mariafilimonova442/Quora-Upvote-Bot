## Introduction
The **Quora Upvote Bot** automates the manual task of finding and upvoting Quora answers to increase reach, engagement, and credibility. It eliminates repetitive actions such as scrolling, locating posts, and tapping upvotes across multiple profiles or devices.

### Automating Quora Engagement Tasks
- Automates finding and upvoting target answers based on keywords or links.  
- Handles multiple Quora accounts for faster, distributed engagement.  
- Works seamlessly across emulators or real devices.  
- Mimics natural user behavior to avoid detection.  
- Perfect for marketers, bloggers, and brands seeking higher Quora visibility.

## How It Works
1. **Input or Trigger** — The automation is launched through the Appilot dashboard, where users define post URLs or keywords for upvoting.  
2. **Core Logic** — Appilot uses UI Automator to navigate the Quora app, locate target posts, and perform upvote actions while simulating human gestures.  
3. **Output or Action** — The system automatically upvotes posts, logs actions, and updates task completion status in real-time.  
4. **Other Functionalities** — Retry handling, proxy management, and device synchronization ensure stable, large-scale operations.

## FAQs
**Q1:** How do I configure multiple Quora accounts?  
A1: Add your accounts in the `accounts.env` file; the bot will auto-rotate sessions during execution.  

**Q2:** Does it support proxy rotation?  
A2: Yes, each account can be assigned a unique proxy for region-based engagement.  

**Q3:** Can I schedule upvotes daily?  
A3: Absolutely — configure schedules in the Appilot dashboard or via the scheduler module.  

**Q4:** Is it detectable by Quora?  
A4: No, the system mimics human activity with randomized inputs and gestures.
